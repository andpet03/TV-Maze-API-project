# ATLAS user guide

## Welcome

ATLAS opens with a changing wall of TV-show covers. Pause it with **Pause covers**. Reduced-motion device settings pause it automatically. If images cannot load, navigation and search still work.

## Your top five

Click ☆ on a show card or **Add to my top five** on a show's detail page. Five ranked slots stay near the top of the app. Use the left/right arrows to change rank and × to remove a favourite. A full ranking must have a show removed before another is added. Favourites and your watchlist are independent.

## Discover

Use **By genre** or **By premiere year**. Choose a category shortcut or use the dropdowns; genre and year filters work together. Sort by rating, premiere date or title. The full catalogue loads automatically on launch. Counts describe loaded data until completion. Complete catalogues are cached in this browser for 24 hours where storage is available. The first load can take several minutes. Interrupted loading shows an error and a retry button; it is never labelled complete. Title search searches TVmaze directly and does not require the catalogue to finish loading.

## Follow a show

Search a title, open the matching show and explore its episodes. Filter by season or watched status. Summaries remain hidden until you enable **Reveal summaries**. Check an aired episode to save your progress; this also adds the show to your watchlist. The next regular episode appears beside the guide.

## Plan an evening

Enter available minutes and press **Plan my evening**. Episodes remain in sequence; an episode that does not fit is never skipped. Unknown runtimes stop planning unless you explicitly enable estimates. Specials are optional.

## Keep your collection

**Backup & restore** exports watchlist, progress and top-five ranking. Restore replaces all three after confirmation. Old version 1 backups still work but have no favourites, so restoring one clears your current top five. There is no account or automatic sync. Keep a backup before changing browsers or website addresses.

## Troubleshooting

If loading fails, check connectivity and retry. A failed catalogue request does not remove already loaded shows. Missing dates, ratings or artwork may be absent in the source data. If the display appears out of date, refresh the page.

## Stable browsing

Stay on a genre or premiere year while more shows download. Existing cards stay in place; newly discovered matching titles are added at the end. Scroll to reveal more. Three animated dots and **Downloading titles…** appear while waiting for more titles. When you intentionally change filters or sorting, the list starts over. Selecting a sort does not allow later downloads to reshuffle the current list.

## Spin the wheel

Choose **Spin the wheel** in the navigation. Enter your available minutes, select a genre (or Any genre) and choose from discovered shows or your watchlist. Press **Spin the wheel**. The result lists a random show's next unwatched regular episode(s), their total runtime and spare time. Open the episode guide to see details and mark episodes watched after viewing. You can spin again for another chance-based result; a repeated suggestion is possible.

Only episodes marked watched in this browser are excluded. The app cannot know viewing history you have not recorded. Missing runtimes are not estimated. If no plan is found among checked shows, try a longer time, another genre or spin again. You may cancel a search. The candidate pool covers shows currently available, not titles still downloading.
